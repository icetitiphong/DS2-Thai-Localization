# Death Stranding 2 project profile

These are this project's user-approved conventions, not universal Thai translation rules. New explicit user choices take precedence.

- Preserve singular I/me/my. ฉัน is the project's default when identity is unconfirmed; it is not evidence of a female speaker. A documented เรา–เจ้า voice may use singular เรา: check context rather than replacing it globally.
- City and song titles stay in English. Country names are a separate category; do not change them automatically.
- Use Carrier for the user-facing floating carrier name, and เอนตัวไปทางซ้าย / เอนตัวไปทางขวา for the approved Shift Balance / Shift Weight action labels. Preserve the source's icon and direction independently. These choices do not redefine every balance action.
- Keep established lore terms: บีช, ไทม์ฟอล, ดรอว์บริดจ์, โกสต์เมค, กระแสทาร์; retain BB, BT, DOOMS, UCA, Ha, Ka and Q-pid. Use a newer project glossary if supplied.
- Sam calling Lou `my kid` requires their relationship and scene, not a global kid-to-child replacement. Exclamations and profanity depend on the actual speaker's register.
- Short requests such as `Don't shoot!` can be corrected without assigning a speaker. `He has my heart` may be literal or figurative; do not decide the referent or medical meaning from the string alone.
- Fan transcripts can provide scene clues, but do not supersede English source text or prove runtime verification. A single transcript paragraph may change recipient; repeated lines may have different speakers.

If the working project contains a character bible, glossary, pronoun policy or scene-speaker register, consult only entries relevant to the selected rows. State when those resources are unavailable; do not claim a complete character bible or UUID mapping from this profile.

This skill does not ship or license a game translation, loader DLL, game assets or transcripts. ZIP and publication are separate requests. If packaging an existing mod, explain unverified redistribution rights and do not silently replace another mod's loader.
